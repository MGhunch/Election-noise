The prompts

How we told the robot to think.

This project uses AI to source, sort and summarise different policies. Here are the prompts we used to get the robot to do its thing.

  • SourceWhere the policies come from, and what gets left out
  • SortHow each one is sized, shaped and placed
  • SummariseHow the one-line description gets written

If you spot something that doesn't look right, just click the flag in the detail window and we'll take another look. Robots can hallucinate, although likely less often than politicians.
